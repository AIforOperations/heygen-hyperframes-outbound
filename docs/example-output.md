# Example output

What a finished video looks like for one prospect.

## Prospect

- **Name:** Jane Smith
- **Role:** VP Marketing
- **Company:** Acme Corp (acme.com)

## Stat we found

Homepage loads in 4.2 seconds. Industry benchmark is under 2.

## Script

> "Hey Jane, quick one for you. Saw you're running marketing at Acme. Pulled up your homepage and it's loading in 4.2 seconds. That's costing you about 30% of mobile visitors before the page even renders. We could pull that under 1.5 seconds in a week. Worth a chat?"

## Hyperframe sequence

| Time | Hyperframe | Content |
|------|-----------|---------|
| 0 to 5s | Intro card | Jane's profile photo + name + "Hey Jane" overlay |
| 5 to 12s | Company panel | Acme logo + tagline + Jane's role badge |
| 12 to 22s | Stat callout | Big "4.2s" with red bar + industry benchmark line |
| 22 to 30s | Fix preview | Before/after speed test mockup |

## End result

30-second video. Avatar talks directly to Jane. Visuals on screen are all about Jane's company. Feels like a custom analyst segment, not a cold pitch.
